FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What exactly is a "manual fare check"?
Unlike automated scrapers, our human experts personally log in to airline booking systems, GDS platforms, and OTAs to verify that the advertised fare is genuinely available and bookable at that exact price — including all taxes and fees.
How quickly do you post deals after finding them?
Typically within 30–60 minutes of manual verification. Pro and Elite subscribers get alerts up to 2 hours before deals are published to the public — giving you a real head start on limited-seat fares.
Do you book the flights for me?
We don't process bookings directly — we show you exactly where and how to book at the deal price (usually directly on the airline website). Elite plan members get 1-on-1 booking consultation assistance.
Are award / miles deals included?
Yes! Pro and Elite subscribers receive both cash fare deals and award ticket sweet spots — including partner redemptions, mileage run opportunities, and transfer bonuses across all major loyalty programs.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel at any time with no penalty. Annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis if cancelled within 30 days. No long-term contracts, no nonsense.
